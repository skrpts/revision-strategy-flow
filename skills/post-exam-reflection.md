## Capability

Walks a student through what went well, what did not, how effective the revision approach proved against the questions actually asked, and what to change next time — separating outcome from process.

## When to Use

- Immediately after an exam, while recall is fresh
- Deciding what to change before the next revision cycle
- Distinguishing a knowledge gap from an exam-technique gap

## Inputs

Exam details, performance assessment, revision approach, difficulties encountered, the revision timetable and the practice question results

## Outputs

Structured reflection naming what to keep, what to change, and the revision behaviours to adjust
